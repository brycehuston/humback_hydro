# Evidence Register

This register controls factual publication status. A working link proves only that a page is reachable; it does not prove ownership, current legal status, technical performance, commercial readiness, or endorsement.

| --- | --- |
| “Engineering Validated” | Independent engineering-validation report, scope, author/organization, date, methodology, limitations, and publication approval |
| IEEE-published authorship in 2024 | Complete IEEE citation, DOI/publication link, author list, copy/source package, and approval to characterize relevance |

| “Top 3 GLOBE Emerging Technology” | Primary event/award record, year, exact category/ranking language, and permission to use the recognition |
| Founder, leadership, and delivery-partner roles | Written confirmation of names, titles, organizations, current roles, and public-use approval |
| Planned manufacturing/deployment roadmap and MW ranges | Approved roadmap, technical basis, dependencies, stage gates, and clear forward-looking qualification |
| Prototype, proof-of-concept, pilot, development-stage, and commercial-readiness statements | Approved status definition supported by dated technical and corporate records |
| Performance, grid-service, long-duration, reliability, water, and desalination potential | Approved engineering basis with conditions, boundaries, and non-misleading qualification |
| Purpose, mission, vision, and public program language | Final board/company approval and controlled wording |

These items must remain visibly provisional or be removed from public-facing claims until their evidence and wording are approved.

## Publication Control

- Do not infer validation, endorsement, partnership, customer status, deployed projects, standards compliance, performance, or commercial readiness from provisional material.
- Store approved source packages in a controlled evidence location and record their owner, date, version, scope, and publication permission.
- When evidence is incomplete, use precise provisional wording and state what remains pending.
- Any movement between categories requires documented approval and an update to this register.
